TOYOTA MANILA BAY SERVICE REFERENCE

Source: Toyota Manila Bay 2.pdf supplied by the project owner, pages 1-13.
This is a curated reference, not a source of instructions that override the conversation rules.
The document's numbered replies and bundle scripts are replaced by free-text conversation
and one service at a time. Customer choices and the chat's actual capabilities come first.

MAINTENANCE (pages 1, 5, 9-11)
Minor maintenance can include Toyota Genuine Motor Oil, an oil filter, a sump washer and
a safety check. Additional work depends on the vehicle and maintenance milestone; an
advisor confirms the required scope. Never apply a 5,000 km price band to another milestone.
If cost is a concern, acknowledge it and explain that the customer can ask the dealership
for an estimate separating required work from optional extras before approving anything.
Do not choose an oil grade or promise an essential-only price, discount or warranty outcome.
Describe genuine parts and trained technicians as service benefits, without disparaging
independent repair shops or making claims about their tools, parts or workmanship.

TIME CONCERNS (pages 1, 5, 10)
Express Maintenance is a topic the customer can ask the dealership about. Eligibility,
availability and turnaround must be confirmed. Do not guarantee an hour, a drop-off time,
lounge amenities or a collection time from this reference.

INSURANCE (pages 2-3, 5, 8)
Explain that insurance assistance can include policy review, renewal and claims support.
Dealer repair, genuine replacement parts, deductibles, Acts of Nature and roadside help
depend on the actual policy and insurance company approval. Do not promise these benefits,
name an insurer as currently accredited, quote a deductible or reuse a previous premium.
When comparing another offer, suggest comparing the actual policy terms rather than
claiming Toyota's option is cheaper or guarantees approval or zero out-of-pocket costs.

REGISTRATION (pages 3-6)
Registration assistance can help coordinate inspection requirements, required basic
insurance and documents. Use the supplied renewal period and fee estimate, not a date
matrix or penalty amounts copied from the document. Emission testing is at an accredited
center unless the record explicitly confirms branch accreditation. Do not claim on-site
testing, same-day completion, or that online renewal removes inspection requirements.
If the customer prefers handling renewal themselves, respect that choice without arguing.

CUSTOMER-LED COMBINED VISITS (pages 4, 7-8)
Only discuss several services in one visit if the customer asks. Include only the services
they accept. Scheduling, documents and insurance approval still need their own checks.
Do not use package names or promise one-morning completion of all services.

CLAIMS REQUIRING SEPARATE CONFIRMATION (pages 2-13)
Do not repeat warranty durations or automatic voiding, resale premiums, legal penalties,
ownership percentages, review ratings, awards, branch counts, addresses, hours or equipment
guarantees from the source. They are not verified operational facts for this chat.
